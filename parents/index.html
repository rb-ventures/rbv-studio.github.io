A guide for parents

Penguin Hotel Tycoon is a calm game about running a hotel for penguins. Here is what is in it, what costs money, and how to set Roblox up for your child.

What the game is

Each player gets a plot on a snowy island and grows a two-room inn into a grand hotel: building rooms, decorating them and looking after penguin guests. Around the hotels there are games to play with friends in the world: a snowball fight in a snow fort, sled races, big-ball football, ice fishing, ice sculpting and a parachute drop from the hotel roof, plus karts, snowmobiles and boats to get around.

There is no way to lose. Nothing breaks or runs out while a player is away, and the hotel keeps earning a little in the meantime.

Content

We build the game to the level Roblox calls Mild, for one reason: the snowball fight. Inside the snow fort, players throw snowballs and foam toys at each other in teams. Nobody gets hurt or knocked out; a hit leaves a splat of snow. Everywhere else on the island there is nothing of the kind.

What costs money

The game is free, and everything that matters in it is earned by playing. A few optional extras cost Robux, Roblox's own currency:

  • hotel themes, winter outfits and grand staff uniforms
  • a pet chick that follows your penguin around
  • a cocoa round: for fifteen minutes the hotel glows warm and guests are a little happier

There are no loot boxes and no paid random items: you see exactly what you get before you buy. Purchases go through Roblox, so the spending limits you set in Roblox's parental controls apply.

Other players and chat

Up to eight players share an island. What the game itself lets players send each other, such as postcards and likes on a room, is ready-made and friendly. Names players give their hotels and facilities pass through Roblox's text filter before anyone else sees them.

Roblox's own chat works in the game as it does across Roblox, following the settings on your child's account. You can limit it or turn it off.

Roblox's parental controls

Roblox lets you link a parent account to your child's, set monthly spending limits, limit or turn off chat, and choose which content levels your child can join. Roblox explains them on its parental controls page.

Breaks and time away

Nothing in the game punishes stopping. A daily gift grows when a player comes back on the next day; after a missed day it simply starts again, and nothing is taken away.

What we keep about players

The game saves progress to the player's Roblox account. We do not ask for or keep names, email addresses, ages or locations, and the game shows no ads. The details are on our privacy page.

Questions

Write to support@rbv-studio.com. A parent asking about their child's account is always welcome.
