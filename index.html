The promises in every game we make

Our games are made to relax in, for players of every age.

  • You always see what you buy

    No loot boxes and no paid surprises: you know exactly what you get before you pay. Extras are small treats and looks, never needed to play.

  • Nothing lost while you are away

    Your hotel keeps going when you log off. Nothing breaks or runs out while you are gone.

  • No pressure

    There is no way to fail and no rush. Play for five minutes or a whole afternoon.

  • Kind by design

    Notes and postcards players send each other are friendly ready-made messages, and names people type go through Roblox's filter.
