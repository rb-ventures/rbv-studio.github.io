Delete your MyPooTime account

How to delete your MyPooTime account and its data, in the app or without it.

In the app (quickest)

Open MyPooTime, then Settings → Account → Delete account. The app explains what is deleted, asks you to confirm, and then asks whether to also erase your progress on this phone.

Without the app

Use our deletion form.

  • With your deletion code. You find it in the app under Settings → Account, and in every export file. Your account is deleted 7 days after the request. Until then, the app shows a notice with a button to cancel it.
  • Without a code. Give your username and an email address. We reply to that address to confirm the account is yours before we delete anything. A username alone never deletes an account.

You can also write to support@rbv-studio.com.

What is deleted, and what is kept

We delete your username, keys, phones, profile, friends, blocks and mutes, invites, map area, leaderboard scores, progress copy and linked sign-ins. In shared chats your messages show as "Deleted user" with the text removed. Some records are kept for a limited time, or longer when the law requires it. The privacy policy lists them under "Deleting your data and your account".

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.

Data on your phone

Your sessions and progress stay on your phone until you erase them: Settings → Data → Erase data on this phone. Users under 13 have no account, so there is nothing on our server to delete; erasing the phone removes everything.
