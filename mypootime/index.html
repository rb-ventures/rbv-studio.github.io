What's in it

We're not saying it will change your life. Your bathroom routine, though, will never be the same.

  • The big button

    Start a session with one tap. Your streak and points tick up, and the app keeps you company.

  • The Poo Map

    See who else is on the throne nearby. Pins are deliberately fuzzy, so nobody's exact spot shows.

  • Quick mini-games

    More than a dozen, from Poo Docku to Royal Pong. Scrolling is for amateurs.

  • Avatars to collect

    Dress your poo mascot in fifty-plus looks, from caps to costumes, bought with points you earn.

  • Throne titles

    Badges for keeping a streak, playing games and generally committing to the bit.

  • Friends and stickers

    Add friends, send stickers mid-session, start a group chat.

  • Your Poofile

    Your stats and titles on a card you can share. Flex responsibly.

  • Poo jokes

    A joke machine, written with AI, for the longer visits. Blame the algorithm.

13+

Made for teenagers and grown-ups

MyPooTime is for ages 13 and up. It has chat with friends and a map of nearby players with deliberately fuzzy locations, and it is supported by ads with an optional premium plan. For younger players we make games like Penguin Hotel Tycoon. More in our guide for parents.

Questions

When can I get it?

Early 2027, on the App Store and Google Play. Follow @mypootime on Instagram to hear first.

Is it free?

Free to download and supported by ads. An optional premium plan will unlock unlimited sessions, extra avatars and custom sounds. Points are earned in the app, never sold.

Wait, is this real?

Very. It's made by RBV-Studio, the small studio behind Penguin Hotel Tycoon, by people who thought a bathroom break deserved a scoreboard.

What about my privacy?

MyPooTime will publish its own privacy policy before it launches, saying exactly what the app keeps. Questions before then: support@rbv-studio.com.
