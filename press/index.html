Penguin Hotel Tycoon on Roblox

Penguin Hotel Tycoon is a cozy tycoon game on Roblox. Players grow a two-room inn on a snowy island into a grand hotel, decorate every room by hand, and look after penguin guests who write letters and make wishes. Between shifts at the front desk, friends play together in the world: snowball fights in a snow fort, sled races, big-ball football, ice fishing and a parachute drop from the roof. There is no way to lose and nothing is lost while you are away.
